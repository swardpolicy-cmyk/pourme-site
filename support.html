Support

Questions, bug reports, feedback, or anything about your account — we read everything.

Email: contact@pourmellc.com

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Reporting content or users

PourMe has a community feed, and we want it to stay worth reading. You can report a review, photo, comment, or user directly in the app from the item itself. Reported content is reviewed promptly, and content that violates our guidelines is removed. You can also block any user in the app to stop seeing their content.

If you'd rather report something by email, or need to follow up on a report, write to contact@pourmellc.com with a link or screenshot.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent and removes your reviews, collection, and account information.

Common questions

  • Is my collection's value visible to others? No. Purchase prices are private by default, and you can also hide them on your own device in Settings.
  • Does label scanning upload my photos? No — scanning runs entirely on your device.
  • Can I export my data? Email us and we'll help.
