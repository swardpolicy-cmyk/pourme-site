Built for how bourbon people actually drink.

Log a pour in two taps, or go deep with batch numbers, barrel picks, and full tasting notes. PourMe keeps up either way.

A shelf, not a spreadsheet

Your collection as a shelf you're proud of — every bottle distinct, fill levels at a glance, killed bottles honored, not deleted.

Scores that tell a story

Rate the same bottle at different fill levels and watch the curve. Does air really change it? Now you'll have your own data.

Blind tastings, no spreadsheet

Run a blind flight solo or with a friend pouring. Score glass A through F, guess what's what, then reveal — and see how your blind score compares to what you said when you knew the label.

Know what a pour costs

That $120 bottle? About $7 a pour. PourMe does the math from price and fill so expensive bottles finally get a fair frame.

A community that pours back.

Follow reviews from people whose palates you trust, save any bottle to your wishlist in one tap, and compare batches with drinkers who've had the same pick. When you finally log a wishlisted bottle, PourMe celebrates and crosses it off for you.

Wishlist from any review

See a review that convinces you? One tap saves the bottle — with a note about whose review sent you hunting.

Batch-level truth

B523 is not C923. Reviews attach to the actual release, so batch arguments come with receipts.
