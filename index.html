Questions, answered

Is PourMe free?

PourMe is free to download and use during launch. Any future premium features will be clearly marked before you ever pay a cent.

Do I need an account?

Yes, and it takes one tap. PourMe uses Sign in with Apple exclusively, so there's no password to create and nothing extra to remember.

Does PourMe sell alcohol?

No. PourMe never sells, ships, or facilitates the purchase of alcohol. It's a tracking and community app for bottles you already own. For blind tastings, participants handle their own pours.

How do blind tastings work remotely?

Everyone sets up their own pours based on the flight's criteria, tastes on their own device, and logs notes blind. When the session ends, the reveal happens for everyone at once.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, at any time from Settings. Deleting your account permanently removes your data from PourMe.

How is the community kept civil?

Every review, comment, and image can be reported in-app, and you can block any user. Reported content is hidden while our team reviews it.
