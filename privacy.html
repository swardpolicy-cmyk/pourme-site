Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — set before publishing]

PourMe is a whiskey tracking app made by PourMe LLC. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your data exists to run your account and the app's features, we don't sell it, and you can delete your account and its data from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your email), along with a unique account identifier.
  • Content you create. Bottles you add, reviews, scores, tasting notes, photos you attach, blind tasting flights, blend recipes, wishlist items, and collection details including fill levels and purchase prices you enter.
  • Pricing signals. If you mark a purchase as "at retail," the price you entered contributes to anonymous, aggregated typical-retail estimates shown to other users. Prices are never shown to other users as yours.
  • Usage and device data. Basic technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, such as device type, app version, and crash data. [VERIFY: list any analytics SDKs in use before publishing.]

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • Label scanning runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos you scan for bottle identification are not uploaded for that purpose.

How your content is shared

You control the visibility of your reviews when you post them. Public reviews appear in the community feed with your profile name. Your purchase prices are private by default and are only visible to others if you choose to share them. Wishlists are private by default.

Where data is stored

PourMe runs on Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Storage), operated by Google LLC, which processes data on our behalf. Data is stored on Google's servers in the United States.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. You can delete your account and its associated data from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account). This removes your account information and content from our systems.
  • Control visibility. Review visibility, price visibility, and wishlist visibility are all adjustable in Settings.
  • Contact us. For questions or requests about your data, email contact@pourmellc.com.

Age

PourMe is intended for people of legal drinking age in their region (21+ in the United States). The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the legal drinking age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new date above.
