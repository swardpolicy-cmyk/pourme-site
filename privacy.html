Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This policy explains what information PourMe collects, how we use it, who we share and sell it to, and the choices and rights you have. PourMe is operated by PourMe LLC ("PourMe," "we," "us").

The short version. We collect the account details and whiskey data you enter, plus device and advertising identifiers. We work with advertising partners, including Meta, to show you targeted ads. We also sell certain user data to whiskey producers, distilleries, and retailers. You can opt out of both, at any time, at no cost — see Your rights and choices.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your email) and a unique account identifier.
  • Content and collection data. Bottles you add, reviews, scores, tasting notes, photos, blind tasting flights, blend recipes, wishlist items, fill levels, purchase prices, and the stores where you bought bottles.
  • Communications. Messages you send us, including support requests and content reports.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and identifier data. Device model, operating system version, app version, language, and advertising identifiers, including Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) where you permit tracking.
  • Usage data. Screens viewed, features used, in-app events, session timing, and crash and diagnostic data.
  • Approximate location. Derived from the stores you tag and, where you grant permission, from your device, to support location-based features.

2. How we use information

  • To operate your account and the app's features.
  • To display and aggregate community content, including reviews and crowd-sourced typical-retail price estimates.
  • To measure app performance, diagnose problems, and improve the product.
  • To deliver and measure advertising, including personalized and targeted advertising through third-party advertising partners.
  • To create data and insight products for whiskey producers, distilleries, and retailers, as described in section 4.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Advertising and tracking

PourMe uses the Meta SDK (Facebook) to deliver and measure advertising. Through it, Meta may receive device and advertising identifiers, app events such as installs, sign-ups, and in-app actions, and related technical data. Meta may combine that information with data it holds about you from other apps and websites to personalize advertising inside and outside PourMe.

On iOS, we ask your permission before tracking you across apps and websites, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, we do not access your IDFA or share identifiers for cross-app tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Meta acts as an independent controller for the data it receives, and its handling of that data is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and selling your information

Service providers

PourMe runs on Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Storage), operated by Google LLC, which processes data on our behalf under contract. Data is stored on servers in the United States.

Advertising partners

As described in section 3, we share device identifiers and app event data with Meta for advertising purposes. Under California law this constitutes both a "sale" and "sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising."

Whiskey producers, distilleries, and retailers

We sell user data to whiskey producers, distilleries, retailers, and their agents, who use it for market research, product development, and marketing. This may include your reviews and ratings, the bottles in your collection and wishlist, purchase prices and locations, tasting preferences, and associated identifiers.

This is a sale of personal information under California law and comparable state privacy laws. You have the right to opt out at any time, at no cost, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising it. See Your rights and choices.

Other disclosures

We may disclose information to comply with law or valid legal process, to protect the rights, safety, or property of PourMe, our users, or the public, and in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

5. Public content

You control the visibility of your reviews when you post them. Public reviews appear in the community feed with your profile name and may be visible to anyone using the app. Purchase prices are private by default and are only visible to others if you choose to share them. Wishlists are private by default.

6. Your rights and choices

Everyone

  • Opt out of the sale and sharing of your data. Use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" control in the app under Settings → Privacy, or email contact@pourmellc.com. This covers both advertising partners and producer data sales.
  • Opt out of tracking. Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or turn tracking off in your device settings.
  • Delete your account. Delete your account and its data from inside the app at Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent.
  • Control visibility. Review, price, and wishlist visibility are all adjustable in Settings.

European Economic Area and UK residents (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, our legal bases are: contract, to operate your account and provide the app; consent, for personalized advertising and for the sale of data to producers, which we request separately and which you may withdraw at any time; legitimate interests, for security, fraud prevention, and product improvement; and legal obligation, where required by law.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Data is transferred to and stored in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Data retention

We retain your account information and content while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information except where retention is required by law. Data already transferred to advertising partners or producers before an opt-out or deletion request is subject to their own retention practices; we will pass deletion requests on to them where the law requires.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Age

PourMe is intended for people of legal drinking age in their jurisdiction (21+ in the United States). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the legal drinking age. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the changes take effect. Where the law requires consent for a change, we will ask for it.
